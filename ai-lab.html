Why this concept is different

Plan around attention, not just deadlines.

Traditional task lists treat every item as equal. FocusFlow adds an effort dimension so a student can match demanding work to periods when they have the most attention.

A simple study rhythm

Make room for your next small win.

01 / Check in

Choose the energy level that fits your moment. Use the focus map to find a starting point, whether you’re ready for deep work or a lighter task.

02 / Make it specific

Pick one clear next step: finish a function, capture three reading notes, or review image descriptions. Give yourself a small, concrete target.

03 / Pause and reset

After a work session, note what you finished and take a break. Check in again before choosing your next task—your plan can change with your energy.
